About Me

I am a second-year PhD student in Physics at Cornell University. My work is in experimental cosmology with experience in instrumentation and data processing for the TIME experiment and ongoing work on KID simulations for CCAT. I love being outdoors - the sun and water change my brain chemistry. When I'm feeling creative, I like painting, crocheting and working on building things. I enjoy exploring new hobbies; I've gotten into climbing, knitting and building computers in the past few months.

TIME Data Pipeline

KID Simulations

Current project involving detector simulations for kinetic inductance detectors (KIDs). Focus on modeling noise, resonator behavior, and performance evaluation.
